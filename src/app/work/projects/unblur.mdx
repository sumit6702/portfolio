## Overview

UNBLUR is a premium product packaging and identity design project showcasing custom illustrations, structural dieline development, and cohesive branding layouts.

## Key Features

- **Packaging Design**: Created sleek, premium glass bottle labels and box mockups.
- **Visual Identity**: Established color palette, solid typography hierarchy, and logo marks.
- **Production Dielines**: Developed print-ready structural dielines in Adobe Illustrator.
