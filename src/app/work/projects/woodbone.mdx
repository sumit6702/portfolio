## Overview

Woodbone is a brand identity project for a premium woodwork studio, blending clean minimalist aesthetics with organic visual depth.

## Key Features

- **Identity System**: Created organic logo marks and geometric icons.
- **Collateral Design**: Designed custom business cards, hangtags, and catalog sheets.
- **Brand Consistency**: Established wood-grain texture overlays and organic styling guides.
